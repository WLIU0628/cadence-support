Privacy Policy

Effective 16 June 2026.

Cadence is an iOS application that stores all data on your device. The developer does not operate a backend, does not transmit any of your data off your device, and does not have access to any of your information.

Data Collected by the Developer

None.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your interval workouts (the seeded library plus any you create or edit).
  • Your run history — per-lap actual durations, outcomes, and (when an HR source is available) per-lap heart-rate samples recorded at roughly 0.2 Hz.
  • Your heart-rate profile: maximum HR, optional resting HR, optional lactate-threshold HR, and your preferred zone model. Only present if you set them in Settings or the on-boarding wizard.
  • The identifier of a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor you have paired (if any), so the app can reconnect automatically.
  • Your preferences — haptic toggle, TTS toggle, voice selection, notification permissions you have granted, etc.

This data lives only in your device's local storage. Removing the app deletes it.

Apple Health

With your explicit permission, Cadence integrates with Apple Health via the HealthKit framework:

  • Reading your live heart rate while a workout is in progress, to display it on-screen and color-code the in-app zone chip.
  • Writing completed workouts to Apple Health as HKWorkoutActivityType.highIntensityIntervalTraining so they contribute to your Activity rings. When you have paired a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor and that sensor produced samples during the run, those samples are attached to the workout so its HR chart in Health is populated.

These read and write operations are mediated entirely by Apple's HealthKit framework. The data never leaves your device in any form the developer can observe.

Bluetooth

With your explicit permission, Cadence connects to a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor advertising the standard Heart Rate Service (0x180D). The information exchanged is limited to heart-rate samples (and the necessary handshake), used only for live display in the app and for the optional Apple Health writeback described above. No information is transmitted off your device.

Cadence does not declare the iOS bluetooth-central background mode by design — if you background the app mid-workout, sensor sample collection pauses until you bring the app forward.

Notifications

With your explicit permission, Cadence schedules local notifications that fire on each lap boundary when the app is backgrounded mid-workout. No remote push tokens are issued; no notifications come from a server.

Analytics, Advertising, Tracking

None. Cadence integrates no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children

Cadence does not knowingly collect information from children. Because it does not collect any information at all, the framework simply does not apply.
